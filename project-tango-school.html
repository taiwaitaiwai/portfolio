Tango School — UX Research & Web Concept

Sinus-Milieu Target Group Profiling, Psychographic Personas & Web Interface Design

Concept overview for the Tango School web experience, balancing cultural depth with energetic community interaction.

Tango School is a web concept and UX research project that explores target audience profiling using Germany's Sinus-Milieu framework. Rather than relying solely on surface-level demographics (age or income), the project analyzes deeply rooted lifestyle habits, social values, aesthetic preferences, and cultural motivators to guide web structure and content hierarchy.

The research synthesizes two opposing user profiles: a reflective, culturally driven user seeking intellectual depth and community connection, and a spontaneous, high-energy user seeking social excitement and creative expression.

Strategic Research & Psychographic Alignment

  • Sinus-Milieu Mapping: Categorized target visitors into specific socio-ecological and hedonistic milieus to understand their real-world motivations beyond simple demographics.
  • Motivation & Lifestyle Profiling: Mapped daily routines, housing environments, social media habits, and personal quotes to build grounded user journeys.
  • Content Strategy & Communication Channels: Tailored website content streams into both in-depth long-form editorial pieces (for reflective cultural seekers) and short-form visual highlights (for spontaneous event seekers).
