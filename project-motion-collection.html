2. EcoTekFutura — 3D Technical Product Explainer

EcoTekFutura specializes in sustainable drain and overflow protection systems. For this commercial explainer, I modeled and rendered the drain assembly in SolidWorks to accurately illustrate mechanical fluid flow and microplastic filtration. 2D vector overlays were created in Adobe Animate, paired with ElevenLabs multi-language AI voice synthesis for international marketing deployment.
