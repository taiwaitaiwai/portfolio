Digital Readymade / VR Gallery

Postdigital 3D Exhibition, Open Web VR Framework & Mobile Cardboard Experience

Exhibition banner framing the postdigital critique around 3D printing culture ("Everyone's a printer, only I'm a maker").

Cheap custom-made prostheses, precious spare parts, local fabrication: 3D printing is a promising technology in many ways. But a look at the top downloaded 3D files on platforms like Thingiverse shows that most people still print gadgetry and novelty items—stuff that could be produced better in traditional mass production or not produced at all. This project points out that flaw by curating a satirical VR museum around the topic.

Rooted in the digital version of the traditional art form "readymade", we based our VR museum on found digital assets (3D models). We rearranged and modified them to give them ironic new meanings, such as stacked toy boats becoming a "leg prosthesis", a whistle modified into an "artificial lung", and a reframed T-Rex skull acting as a "one-of-a-kind shoe".

Technology & My Contributions

The entire experience is built with the Open Web VR Framework A-Frame (built on top of Three.js). Models were sourced from Thingiverse, modified in Blender, and placed inside a custom multi-story 3D scene representing an active Makerbot 3D printer.

  • Concept Development: Defined the postdigital narrative framework and sarcastic art naming conventions.
  • 3D Modeling & CAD: Prepared and optimized 3D files in Blender, balancing polycount performance with web fidelity.
  • A-Frame Engineering: Coded gaze-based interaction triggers (looking at models rotates or floats them; looking at labels reveals titles).
