Ägyptisches Museum Leipzig Trailer

2D Retro-Adventure Animation, Stop-Motion Workflow & Museum Concept Trailer

Key frame from the 2D retro adventure animation featuring Georg Steindorff's expedition setup.

MISSION: Die Steindorff-Expedition is a 60–70 second promotional trailer conceptualized for the Egyptian Museum (Georg Steindorff) at the University of Leipzig. Designed to engage children (ages 6–12) and families, the trailer turns historical museum heritage into an exciting level-based 2D side-scrolling adventure game.

The visual style blends retro 2D adventure game aesthetics with stop-motion animation principles, combining illustrated characters (such as expedition founder Georg Steindorff) with authentic photographs of real museum artifacts.

Concept & Exposé Breakdown

  • Core Message: Georg Steindorff laid the foundation for a world-class collection through historic expeditions. Today, this collection in Leipzig's Krochhochhaus is waiting for YOU (the new player) to explore.
  • Prologue (1840): Begins in 1840 on ancient papyrus background, showing Gustav Seyffarth examining a sarcophagus, transitioning to 1893.
  • Main Game (1893): Georg Steindorff rides a camel across the desert in a 2D side-scrolling level, collecting artifacts.
  • Collection Level-Up: Collecting items triggers dynamic pop-ups featuring real high-resolution photos of museum treasures.
  • Modern Transition: A time-jump graphic transitions from Steindorff's era directly to the Krochhochhaus museum entrance in modern Leipzig, where a child steps up as the "New Player".
