Authentic Thai Cuisine Photography

Visual Composition, Moodboards, AI Pre-Visualization & Full-Frame Food Styling

Hero flatlay shot featuring hand-ground green curry paste, galangal, lemongrass, and kaffir lime leaves arranged on a bamboo mat backdrop.

This culinary photography project focuses on crafting an authentic, vibrant visual narrative for an artisan Thai cookbook ("Echt Thai. Echt gut."). The visual strategy centers around a natural color palette—vibrant greens from fresh Thai herbs, deep natural blues from butterfly pea flowers, creamy coconut whites, and subtle red chili accents.

The workflow spanned the full production pipeline: persona research, moodboard curation, AI-assisted shot composition planning, physical prop/food styling, full-frame camera shooting with natural light setups, and final color grading in Adobe Lightroom.

Production Pipeline & Setup

  • Audience Curation: Developed target personas (Linh & Jonas) seeking uncompromised, cultural authenticity rather than westernized recipe adaptations.
  • Moodboard & Prop Selection: Sourced authentic props including traditional Thai ceramic dishware, bamboo placemats, stone mortars, and butterfly pea flowers.
  • AI Pre-Visualization: Utilized generative AI to simulate camera framing, depth-of-field, and lighting setups prior to the physical shoot to optimize studio time.
  • Compositional Geometry: Executed shots utilizing classical artistic rules such as the Rule of Thirds, diagonal leading lines, and Golden Spiral compositions.
  • Post-Production: Performed color correction and depth enhancement in Adobe Lightroom to accentuate raw textures, vibrant herb tones, and natural highlights.
