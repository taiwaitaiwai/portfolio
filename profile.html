It’s nice to meet you!

I am a curious creative who bridges the gap between high-level interface strategy and the down-to-earth technical execution of code. Originally from Hong Kong and now calling Berlin home, my journey has been a search for the perfect intersection between human experience and digital performance.

My background is a diverse tapestry of design disciplines. I’ve developed OEM designs for the global automotive sector, analyzed market trends in the Asian gaming industry, and illustrated the future of interfaces through a Master of Arts at FH Potsdam.

Currently, I am strengthening my technical foundation at Okidu, where I translate UI concepts into clean, responsive HTML and CSS. I believe that a great interface isn't just a screen—it’s a meaningful solution that balances business goals with human connection.

When I’m not deep in Figma or code, you’ll likely find me exploring food photography, experimenting with 2D animation, or staying active at the gym.
