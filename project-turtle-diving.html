Turtle Diving Resort

Responsive One-Pager, Interactive Map & After Effects Video Banner

Turtle Diving Resort e-commerce and booking platform interface across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.

Turtle Diving Resort is a responsive B2C one-pager website concept designed for a premier diving resort on Gili Air, Indonesia. The goal of the project was to craft an immersive digital experience that seamlessly blends travel booking, PADI course registrations, marine conservation awareness, and interactive dive spot exploration.

I managed the full end-to-end design and technical planning process: creating the information architecture, wireframing in Balsamiq, establishing WCAG 2.1 AA accessible color systems, prototyping full high-fidelity screen designs in Figma, producing custom vector badge icons, and animating a 30-second ambient motion background in After Effects.

My Process & Contributions

  • Information Architecture: Developed a structured sitemap mapping 7 core one-page sections (Hero, Welcome, Dive Spots, Courses, Booking, About, Contact).
  • UX Wireframing: Designed low-fidelity wireframes establishing 12-column layout grids, spacing tokens (100px section padding), and interactive modal logic.
  • High-Fidelity UI Design: Built full 1440px desktop screen layouts in Figma utilizing custom typography scale (Raleway + Nunito Sans) and marine branding colors (#004282, #276FB7, #EDF2F7).
  • Custom Vector Assets: Created 5 collectible-style dive spot badge icons in Illustrator (Coral Fan Garden, Deep Turbo, Manta Point, Shark Point, Turtle Heaven).
  • Motion Graphics: Edited and rendered a 30s 1080p seamless ambient video loop in After Effects with parallax depth layers and light ray effects.
  • Technical Handoff & Validation: Implemented clean, semantically valid HTML5/CSS3 and client-side Vanilla JavaScript for modal overlays and booking validation.

What I Learned

Designing Turtle Diving Resort reinforced the importance of systematic design thinking. By strictly adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios (achieving an 8.87:1 ratio between dark blue and ice blue backgrounds) and defining modular spacing rules early, the conversion from wireframe to interactive Figma mockup felt fluid and precise. It also allowed me to combine UI engineering with motion design to enhance user engagement above the fold.

4. Motion Banner Production (After Effects)

To create an atmospheric, underwater entry point above the fold, I built a 30-second Full HD (1080p) looping video background in Adobe After Effects. The animation separates underwater photography into parallax depth layers, applying wave warp displacements, animated light rays, and a seamless 1-second opacity crossfade.
